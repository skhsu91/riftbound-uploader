Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Riftbound Uploader is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the Keishun YouTube channel, who runs it on his own computer to manage his own channel.

YouTube API Services

Riftbound Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it accesses the authorized YouTube channel only to: upload videos; set their title, description, tags, language, recording date, audience and disclosure settings; add them to the channel's own playlists; set a thumbnail; make them public or schedule them; and post a comment and a like on the channel's own videos. It reads the channel's identity, playlists and video status to do those things.

What it stores, and where

It stores one Google sign-in token, in the macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. It stores nothing else. It has no server, no database, no analytics, and uses no cookies.

Sharing

No information is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. Videos and their details go only to YouTube, which is the tool's whole purpose.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Contact

Questions or complaints about privacy: contact the owner through the Keishun YouTube channel.
